        Review

        How Review works

        Get a question right and it comes back after 3 days, then 7, 16 and 35 days while you keep getting it right. Miss it and it comes back the next day. Spacing your practice out like this helps you remember for longer than doing it all at once.

        Right three times in a row, days apart, and a question counts as mastered.

        Add an exam date and Review paces itself to it: questions you’re still learning come back closer together as the day gets near, every question gets a last look in the days before it, and the ones you haven’t tried come in a few a day.

          Questions

          What are points?

          Points pay for the work the AI does. An answer from RamzAI, a research report, a summary or a quiz is 1 point; a whole slide deck drafted by RamzAI is 3; a research writing (ten statements for manual research writing, or a draft from automatic research writing) is 2. Each pass comes with its points, and they end when the pass ends. Points you get for inviting friends, and points bought on their own before, never end.

          How much research can I do?

          Every Scholar pass opens RamzAI’s Research mode and research writing (automatic and manual). On Month, up to 10 of the 100 points can go on research. On Half year, up to 60 of the 620. Year has no research limit. Points you don’t use on research work on everything else in RamzAI. Free has 2 research writings a month.

          Why not everything free?

          The AI costs Academic-Home money every time it works. So a few points each month are free, and the rest is paid for by the month, the half year or the year. Everything that runs on your phone itself stays free.

          Does anything renew by itself?

          No. You pay once for the time you chose, and you decide if and when to pay again. If you buy a new pass while one is still running, its days and points are added on top.

          Is there a limit in a day?

          A pass spends at most 60 points in one day. That keeps a lost phone or a runaway script from using up a whole year in an afternoon. The count starts again at midnight.

          Are my files kept?

          Not on our server. Files you convert or edit are processed, sent straight back to you, and then deleted from our server. A copy stays on your own device, in Your files.
