Definitions
In this document, these words have the meanings below.

service noun — the Academic-Home app, its website and everything they do.

account noun — your registered place in Academic-Home, identified by your email address and protected by your password.

content noun — the files, text, notes, slides and other material you put into Academic-Home or create with it.

Scholar noun — the paid part of Academic-Home: the work the AI does for you, every design in Slide Studio, research writing beyond Free’s 2 a month, long meetings and PDF protection. Everything that runs on your device itself is free.

pass noun — Scholar for a fixed time, paid once: a Month (30 days), a Half year (182 days) or a Year. Each pass comes with AI points, and nothing renews by itself. Passes bought earlier for a day, a week or a semester keep working until they end.

points noun — what the AI's work costs inside the app: an answer from RamzAI, a research report, a summary or a quiz is 1 point; a deck drafted by RamzAI is 3; a research writing (ten statements for manual research writing, or a draft from automatic research writing) is 2; a long piece of automatic research writing is written in parts, and each part is 2; the plan for a manual research writing is 1. A pass's points end with the pass; points you get for inviting friends, and points bought on their own earlier, never end.

research points noun — the part of a Month or Half year pass’s points that may be used on research (RamzAI’s Research, and automatic and manual research writing). They are not extra points: points you don’t use on research work on everything else.

subscription noun — the older arrangement, for accounts that bought Pro through the App Store or Google Play before 4.0: it runs to its end date and is then replaced by a pass if you want one.

referral code noun — a short code you share so that, when a friend joins Academic-Home with it, you receive points.

AI abbreviation — artificial intelligence: computer systems that produce answers or text in a way that resembles human reasoning — in Academic-Home, RamzAI and the writing tools.

we, us, our pronouns — Academic-Home and the people who run it. you, your: the person using Academic-Home.

Academic integrity
Academic-Home helps you learn, write and present — it doesn't replace your own work. Follow your school's or institution's rules on AI tools, check and rewrite AI-drafted text in your own voice, and cite properly. Our writing tool never makes up citations, but you are responsible for checking every source before you submit work. We can't promise any institution will accept work made with AI help.
Meetings
Hosts decide who comes in: share a meeting's link only with the people you mean to invite, and keep the waiting room on for a link that might travel. Everyone in a meeting treats the others with respect — no harassment, nothing illegal or harmful — and doesn't record or share a meeting, or anyone's voice or picture, without the agreement of the people in it (Academic-Home doesn't record meetings, but other people's devices could). Meetings have limits that keep them working for everyone: how many people can join one meeting, how long a meeting can last on your plan, and how much meeting time everyone together can use each month. When a limit is reached, a meeting can't start or comes to an end, and the app says so. Hosts can remove anyone from their meeting, and we can end meetings or suspend accounts that break these terms.
AI answers can be wrong
AI can misunderstand, leave things out or be confidently wrong. Check anything important against your course materials or a trusted source — especially for exams, clinical placements and professional work. Academic-Home is a study aid: it is not medical, clinical, legal or financial advice, and must never be used to make decisions about real patients.

Plans and billing
These are free, always: converting files, the PDF and Word editors, scanning, Slide Studio with its classic designs, the focus timer and sounds, quizzes you write yourself. The AI is paid for with points. Free accounts get 5 AI points every month, once per device, including up to 2 research writings (a research report counts as one). Scholar is bought as a pass, paid once with Paystack (by card, or mobile money in Ghana; charged in Ghana cedis, which your bank converts if your card uses another currency), at the price shown before you pay: a Month (30 days), a Half year (182 days) or a Year, each with its points. Every Scholar pass opens RamzAI’s Research and research writing (automatic and manual). Of a Month pass’s 100 points, up to 10 may be used on research; of a Half year pass’s 620 points, up to 60; a Year pass has no research limit. Points not used on research work on every other AI feature. On Free, up to 2 research writings a month. Points you get for inviting friends, and points bought on their own earlier, never end. Nothing renews by itself; buying a pass while one is running adds its days and points on top. A pass spends at most 60 points in one day, so a lost phone or a runaway script cannot use up a year; the count starts again at midnight. Prices may change; a pass already paid for is never changed. Pro bought earlier through a store or for a year keeps running to its end date, with its monthly allowance of RamzAI messages. A meeting can last up to 1 hour on Free and up to 5 hours with Scholar; RamzAI's notes of a meeting use one point. If the AI fails to answer, the points for that request are given back.
Refunds and cancelling
Refunds for store purchases are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies — we can't issue them directly. For a Paystack payment, email us and we'll look at it with Paystack. A pass has nothing to cancel: it simply ends on its last day. If you still have an older store subscription, cancelling it stops future renewals and you keep it until the end of the period already paid for; deleting your Academic-Home account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel it in the store too.
Referral codes
Every account has its own code, and you can also make one code a month that lasts 14 days. When a friend joins Academic-Home with your code, you get 10 points that never end; up to 2 of them may be used on research. A friend counts once: when they have confirmed their email on a phone that had no Academic-Home account before, or the first time they buy a pass with your code. Up to 2 friends count in a month. If you already have an account, you can enter your friend’s code once, in Invite friends, so nobody needs to sign up again. A free month earned before this rule still starts as it was promised. Codes are for sharing with people you know — don't sell them or use fake accounts; codes used that way may be cancelled and the points removed.
